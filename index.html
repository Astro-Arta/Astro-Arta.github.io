NGC-001 · ABOUT

About Me

I'm an M.Sc. Physics student at Sharif University of Technology in Tehran, Iran. My current thesis focuses on the integrated Sachs–Wolfe (ISW) effect in the local limit of non-local gravity, under the supervision of Prof. Shant Baghram.

My broader research interests include extrasolar planet detection, planet formation, protoplanetary disks, and the development of open-source software for astronomical data analysis.

  • Based in Tehran, Iran
  • Current degree M.Sc. Physics
  • Research focus Cosmology & Astrophysics
